# Content Strategy & Website Content — Vedant Exports International

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the content structure, messaging, copy direction, content rules, and reusable content templates for the Vedant Exports International website.

The purpose is to ensure that the website communicates the company consistently across:

- Homepage
- About
- Products
- Product detail pages
- Sourcing
- Cold storage
- Quality
- Certifications
- Export process
- Markets
- Industries
- Request a Quote
- Contact
- FAQ
- Insights

The content must support the primary business objective:

> **Build trust with international B2B buyers and convert qualified visitors into export inquiries.**

This document is a content specification, not a final legal/compliance approval.

---

# 2. Content Principles

The website content should be:

- Professional
- Clear
- International
- B2B-focused
- Specific where facts are available
- Concise
- Credible
- Human
- Confident without exaggeration
- Commercially useful
- SEO-friendly without keyword stuffing

The content should communicate capability rather than make unsupported claims.

### Core principle

> **Show capability. Do not manufacture credibility.**

For example, the website may state that the business has access to North Indian agricultural sourcing networks because this is established project context.

It should not claim:

- "20+ years of export experience"
- "Trusted by thousands of global buyers"
- "Serving 50+ countries"
- "Guaranteed quality"
- "Best prices in India"

unless those claims are independently verified and approved.

---

# 3. Brand Positioning

## 3.1 Primary positioning

Vedant Exports International should be presented as:

> **A modern international agricultural sourcing and export company backed by 20+ years of family agricultural trading experience, established North Indian sourcing networks, storage infrastructure, and professional export capabilities.**

The important distinction is:

- The family/domestic agricultural trading business has 20+ years of experience.
- Vedant Exports International is the newer international/export division.
- Vedant Exports International has approximately 1.5+ years of international/export business experience.

The website must preserve this distinction.

---

# 4. Core Brand Message

The central business proposition is:

> **Sourcing + Quality + Storage + Export Execution**

Supporting message:

> Buyers can approach Vedant with specific product requirements, and the company can coordinate sourcing, quality, packaging, documentation, and logistics according to feasible buyer specifications.

The website should make Vedant feel like a capable sourcing and export partner rather than simply another product trader.

# 7. Tone of Voice

## 7.1 Desired tone

The tone should be:

**Professional + direct + trustworthy + modern + commercially aware.**

The writing should sound like an international B2B company.

### Use

- "We source"
- "We coordinate"
- "We supply"
- "We work with"
- "Buyer-specific requirements"
- "Subject to product availability"
- "According to destination and specifications"
- "Request a quote"

### Avoid

- "We are the world's best..."
- "Guaranteed lowest price"
- "100% guaranteed quality"
- "Unbeatable"
- "No.1 exporter"
- "Revolutionary"
- "Premium solutions for all your needs"
- Excessive corporate jargon
- Artificially enthusiastic marketing language

---

# 8. Messaging Hierarchy

The website should communicate information in this order:

```text
1. What Vedant does
        ↓
2. What products Vedant supplies
        ↓
3. Why a buyer should consider Vedant
        ↓
4. How sourcing/export coordination works
        ↓
5. What evidence supports the claims
        ↓
6. How the buyer can start a conversation
```

The visitor should understand the business quickly without reading large blocks of text.

# 30. Product Content Rules

Product pages must not invent specifications.

For missing information use:

```text
To be confirmed based on buyer requirement.
```

or:

```text
Available specifications can be shared during inquiry.
```

Do not fabricate:

- Nutritional values
- Certifications
- Lab results
- Shelf life
- Moisture percentage
- Pesticide limits
- Grade standards
- Origin claims
- Organic status
- Halal/Kosher status
- Regulatory compliance

unless verified for that product.
